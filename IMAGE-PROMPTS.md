# Image prompts for DoctorHerb

Make each image in any AI image tool (Bing Image Creator, Leonardo, Gemini, Canva Magic Media). Save the files **with exactly these names** in the `images` folder. The site works without them, so you can add them later.

Rules for every prompt: realistic photograph style, natural daylight, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Check every face and plant looks natural before using it.

## 1. images/hero.jpg  (wide, 1600 x 900, JPG under 400 KB)
> Realistic wide photograph of an elderly Zimbabwean woman in a simple headscarf kneeling in a green garden at sunrise, gently holding a leafy medicinal herb plant in her hands, a young granddaughter beside her looking at it with curiosity, soft golden light, shallow depth of field, calm and trustworthy mood, space on the left side of the frame for text, no text

## 2. images/empty.jpg  (square, 800 x 800)
> Realistic top-down photograph of fresh green medicinal leaves and roots laid neatly on a woven reed mat, a small clay pot beside them, soft daylight, clean and tidy, no text

## 3. images/share-preview.jpg  (1200 x 630, used when the link is shared on WhatsApp)
> Realistic close-up photograph of hands holding a fresh green herb sprig in front of a blurred village garden, warm morning light, navy blue and green colour tones, empty space on the right side, no text

## 4. Logo (optional)
> Simple flat vector logo of a two-leaf sprout in amber on a deep navy rounded square, minimal, no text, high contrast

## Tips
- Compress photos at squoosh.app before saving; large images slow down phones on mobile data.
- Use only images you generated or own. Do not copy photos from Google.
- share-preview.jpg is already linked in index.html with your full GitHub Pages address, which WhatsApp needs for link previews.
